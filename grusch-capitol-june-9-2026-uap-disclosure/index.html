The June 9 Capitol Steps Event

David Grusch is scheduled to take center stage at 1:00 PM on the steps of the U.S. Capitol alongside four bipartisan members of Congress. Representatives Eric Burlison, Jared Moskowitz, Anna Paulina Luna, and Tim Burchett will join Grusch in issuing a direct call to President Trump to release classified files and to Congress to pass formal disclosure legislation.

The event comes against a backdrop of unprecedented global momentum. Over 1 billion worldwide users have accessed government-released UAP records following a declassification directive Trump issued in May 2026. Media interest has reached saturation — the topic is trending globally, capturing attention far beyond traditional UFO enthusiast circles.

"President Trump now has an historic opportunity; this press conference is about moving from testimony to action," Grusch stated ahead of the event. "Let the American people judge the facts for themselves." Investigative journalist Leslie Kean and documentary filmmaker James Fox will host the event, with Fox framing the stakes: "The public deserves real answers, Congress deserves access, and reality should not be classified. If our requests are fulfilled, this could change the course of history."

What Grusch Will Say

Grusch plans to use his Capitol appearance to connect dots between his prior congressional testimony and the need for executive action. In July 2023, he testified under oath before the House Committee on Oversight and Accountability about alleged multi-decade crash retrieval programs operating outside normal congressional oversight.

The former National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency officer has stated he interviewed over 40 individuals with firsthand knowledge of retrieval operations and identified specific locations where materials allegedly remain stored — information he says has been provided to the Intelligence Community Inspector General and congressional intelligence committees. That is not vague whistleblowing. That is a documented paper trail inside official channels.

The Three Concrete Demands

The June 9 press conference will formalize three specific asks:

  1. Presidential declassification of specific UAP files Grusch has identified by name to intelligence oversight bodies
  2. Congressional passage of the UAP Disclosure Act — formal legislation with systematic release pathways and national security protections
  3. Strengthened whistleblower protections preventing administrative retaliation, plus international coordination encouraging allied governments to contribute UAP evidence

Rep. Eric Burlison's framing is the sharpest: "President Trump is the first president to take concrete steps towards transparency on UAP. The recent releases are an important start, so now it's the obligation of Congress to make sure career intelligence officials don't obstruct his directive, in order to prevent selective disclosure or another dead end."

The Institutional Resistance

Congressional members have previously been denied access to classified briefings on UAP matters. Grusch has documented retaliation he characterizes as "administrative terrorism" — career attacks, clearance complications, and professional isolation deployed against those pursuing transparency. The 2024 National Defense Authorization Act included UAP-specific whistleblower protections, a legislative acknowledgment that secrecy has suppressed legitimate testimony.

What Comes Next

Whether June 9 produces formal executive response or another round of compelling testimony that disappears into institutional silence is the only question that matters. What has already changed is that denying the question's legitimacy — once standard government posture — has become diplomatically impossible.
