About this site

ChemaVX

I'm ChemaVX. I've spent my whole life looking up at the sky with more questions than answers.

I grew up reading UFO magazines and staying up late listening to late-night radio shows dedicated to the unexplained. I became a devoted X-Files fan at a time when believing in this stuff still earned you strange looks. What I learned early on is that rigor and mystery aren't incompatible — that you can investigate the unexplained without sacrificing seriousness or enthusiasm.

One summer in the Canary Islands, I saw something I haven't been able to explain since. A large, silent light that shot upward into the atmosphere at a speed no aircraft or satellite I know of could reach. It lasted seconds. I have no proof. But I know what I saw.

From that day on, I stopped being just an enthusiast and became someone who needs to understand.

The Exclusion Zone was born from that need.

This isn't a conspiracy blog, and it isn't a site that accepts every story without questioning it. We investigate each case using available sources, present what is known and what isn't, and let you decide. Because the truth about the UAP phenomenon — whatever it turns out to be — deserves more than sensationalist headlines.

I'm convinced that major revelations are coming. The U.S. Congressional hearings, the military testimonies under oath, the declassified programs — something is changing. And when it happens, I want The Exclusion Zone to be the place where you find well-documented information, without noise, without filler.

Welcome to the zone.

— ChemaVX
